# Playlist recipes

Oct 9, 2026

65 recipes built from 326 of your Apple Music playlists, each with an Instant Playlist prompt and Song Alchemy seeds taken from the songs that showed up most across the original playlists.

Recipes for the albums you're adding through Tidarr now are in New library recipes.

## How to use a recipe

Paste the prompt into **Instant Playlist**, or put the seeds into **Song Alchemy** under Include. The prompt gives more variety; the seeds stay closer to the original sound.

- **Based on** lists how many of your old playlists fed the recipe and a few of their names, so you know what it's aiming at.
- **Seeds** are written as Song (Artist). Search each one in Alchemy by title. If one isn't in your library, swap in another song by that artist from the same era.
- **Change the size freely.** "40 songs" and "two hours long" both work. A time only counts when "long" or "of" comes right after it ("two hours long", "two hours of indie"). A bare "two hours," is ignored.
- **Leave the Number of songs box alone.** When the prompt names a size, the prompt wins and the box is ignored. The box only matters when the prompt has no size; then it sets the count (default 50, up to 200). A time becomes a song count at about 3 minutes a song, then AudioMuse trims the list to fit your real track lengths, so "two hours long" lands close to two hours.
- **How the prompts were sized from your playlists.** Each prompt names the 10 artists that show up in the most of its source playlists, plus the two staple songs from its Alchemy seeds. Its length is the 75th-percentile length of those playlists (80 to 200 songs). The per-artist cap is how many songs the busiest artist typically had in one of those playlists, scaled to that length (3 to 6). Named artists pull in similar artists too, so the variety goes beyond the list.
- **More names don't help past this point.** AudioMuse makes at most 4 searches per request with up to 5 seeds each, so 10 artists and 2 songs is about all one prompt can use. To change the mix, swap names rather than adding more.
- **Faith recipes** name Christian artists and say "Christian artists only", because AudioMuse can't tell faith music by sound. Expect to delete a stray secular song now and then.
- **Save what works.** When a run comes out right, save it as a playlist so you aren't relying on the next run matching it.

To recreate one specific playlist instead, use its own song-list prompt in Apple Playlist Prompts. Both approaches are explained in Recreating playlists.
