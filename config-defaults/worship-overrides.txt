# Worship overrides for the Christian artists list.
#
# Most artists are either worship or not, in full - this file is for the
# exceptions: one album or track by an otherwise non-worship Christian
# artist that counts as worship anyway (or vice versa).
#
# Artist | Album                whole album counts as worship
# Artist | Album | Title        just one track
# !Artist | Album               NOT worship, even if the artist is worship=yes
#
# Case, "The ", "&" vs "and" and punctuation don't matter - same rules as
# the artist list. Track beats album beats the artist's own worship flag.
# Lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# Example (commented out):
# for KING & COUNTRY | Burn the Ships | Priceless
